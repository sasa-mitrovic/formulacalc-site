Privacy Policy

FormulaCalc · last updated 3 September 2026

In short: FormulaCalc has no servers and collects nothing. Your library stays on your device. If you switch on Google Drive sync, a copy goes to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no account to create.

What FormulaCalc stores, and where

Everything you enter — raw materials, formulas, notes, categories, suppliers — is written to a single file on the device you are using. That file belongs to you. The app works completely offline, and nothing leaves the device unless you turn on Google Drive sync.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, FormulaCalc stores your library as a file called formulacalc-library.json in your own Drive, so that another device signed in to the same account can read it.

  • FormulaCalc requests one permission, drive.file. That grants access only to files the app itself created. It cannot see, read, or change anything else in your Drive.
  • Nothing syncs on its own. A sync happens when you press the sync button, and the app tells you what it did.
  • Your file is stored in your Drive under your Google account, governed by Google's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it and no copy of it.

Sign-in credentials

When you connect Drive, Google issues FormulaCalc a token so it can sync without asking you to sign in every time. That token is kept in your operating system's secure credential store — DPAPI on Windows, the Android Keystore on Android — on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere except back to Google, and choosing Disconnect Drive deletes it.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No account, no email address, no password.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or device identifiers.
  • Nothing is shared with or sold to any third party, because nothing is collected.

The only network destination FormulaCalc ever contacts is Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com), and only to sign you in and sync your file.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: uninstalling FormulaCalc removes its local library file.
  • In Drive: delete formulacalc-library.json from your Google Drive yourself, like any other file.
  • Revoking access: choose Disconnect Drive in the app, and if you want to be thorough, remove FormulaCalc at your Google account's third-party access page.

Children

FormulaCalc is a tool for perfumery formulation and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
