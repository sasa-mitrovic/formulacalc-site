FormulaCalc

A perfumery formulation notebook for Windows and Android.

FormulaCalc keeps a library of raw materials and the formulas built from them. It does the arithmetic a perfumer actually needs: scaling a formula by weight or by concentration, trading free solvent to change strength, working out a dilution, and showing every formula a material has been used in and at what level.

What it does

  • Raw materials with categories, suppliers, dilutions, cost and IFRA limits.
  • Formulas in weight, with absolute and relative percentages side by side.
  • Six scaling operations, including concentration scaling against free solvent.
  • Accord nesting: replace one entry with a whole formula, in its own proportions.
  • Export to PDF, Markdown, TSV, and a CSV that imports back unchanged.
  • Optional sync through your own Google Drive.

Where your work lives

On your device. FormulaCalc keeps your library in a single file, and it works entirely offline. If you turn on Google Drive sync, that same file is copied to your Drive so a second device can read it — nowhere else, and never automatically. Sync happens when you press the button.

Privacy

FormulaCalc has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. The only service it ever contacts is Google, and only to sync the file you asked it to sync. The full details are in the privacy policy.
